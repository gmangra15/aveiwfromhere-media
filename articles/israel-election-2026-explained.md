Israel holds a general election on **Tuesday, 27 October 2026**, eight months into its war with Iran. The vote is widely seen as a referendum on Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.

## How the system works

All **120 seats** in the Knesset, Israel's parliament, are elected by nationwide proportional representation. A government needs the support of at least **61** members. No single party has ever won that many seats alone, so every Israeli government is a coalition.

## The main players

According to Al Jazeera:

- **Likud**, led by Netanyahu, with his traditional partners Itamar Ben-Gvir (Jewish Power) and Bezalel Smotrich (Religious Zionism), plus the ultra-Orthodox parties Shas and United Torah Judaism.
- **Yashar**, led by former army chief **Gadi Eisenkot**, seen as the most likely opposition candidate for prime minister.
- **Together**, an alliance of **Naftali Bennett** and **Yair Lapid**.
- Other opposition parties, the Democrats and Yisrael Beytenu, which signed a non-aggression pact with Yashar and Together.
- Arab-led parties: **Ra'am**, led by Mansour Abbas, and the **Joint List**.

## What the polls say

Polls cited by Al Jazeera project the opposition bloc at about **52 seats** and Netanyahu's alliance at about **50**, with the Joint List and Ra'am at around **13** combined. On those numbers, neither camp reaches 61 on its own, which makes coalition-building after the vote decisive.

## Possible outcomes

Al Jazeera outlines several scenarios, including:

1. Netanyahu forms another government with existing or new partners.
2. An opposition-led coalition takes power.
3. A government that relies on Ra'am, or on outside support from Arab-led parties.
4. A national unity government, or a rotating premiership like the Bennett-Lapid deal of 2021.
5. No agreement, and another election, with Netanyahu staying on as caretaker.

## The shadow of war

Netanyahu warned on Sunday that Iran or its proxies could try to disrupt the vote, saying the army "is ready both to defend and to strike", AP reported. Meanwhile, delegations from Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Türkiye were in Tehran pushing for a US-Iran deal.

Whoever forms the next government will face decisions on the war with Iran, on Gaza, where the UN warns of worsening hunger, and on relations with Washington, with the US holding its own midterm elections a week later.
