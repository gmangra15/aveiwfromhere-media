France has imposed night curfews in several of its biggest cities after weekend protests turned violent, in what the prime minister described as some of the worst rioting the country has seen in recent years.

## How big were the protests?

About 550,000 people joined protests across France on Saturday, according to French media reports carried by Xinhua. Several cities saw looting and violence over the weekend.

## Where the curfews apply

- **Paris:** from 11 pm to 6 am around Place de la République and streets near Les Halles.
- **Lyon:** from 9 pm in part of the Presqu'île city-centre area.
- **The Nord department** (Lille, Wattignies, Douai and Denain): a two-day curfew from 8 pm, imposed by the prefect with the mayors' agreement.
- **Nice:** a curfew for minors from 9 pm, announced by the mayor.

## Arrests and injuries

According to France's Interior Ministry, 2,397 people were arrested on Sunday. It said 997 police officers and gendarmes were injured, seven of them seriously.

The prime minister has said new curfews will be imposed wherever necessary.

## Why it matters

The unrest comes just six months before France's presidential election, adding pressure on a government already facing deep public frustration. How quickly order returns, and whether the curfews hold, will shape the political mood heading into the campaign.
