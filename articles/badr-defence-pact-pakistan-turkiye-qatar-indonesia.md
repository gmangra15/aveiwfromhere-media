Pakistan, Türkiye, Qatar and Indonesia signed a defence cooperation memorandum in Doha on Wednesday, 21 October. Türkiye's defence ministry has named it the **Badr** Memorandum of Understanding.

## Who signed

- **Pakistan:** Defence Minister Khawaja Asif
- **Türkiye:** Defence Minister Yaşar Güler, with Haluk Görgün, head of the Secretariat of Defence Industries, in attendance
- **Qatar:** Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of State for Defence Affairs Sheikh Saoud bin Abdulrahman Al Thani
- **Indonesia:** Deputy Defence Minister Yusuf Jauhari

## What it covers

According to Türkiye's defence ministry, as reported by Reuters, the memorandum promotes cooperation in defence production and capabilities. It also covers military exercises and training, research and development, and joint work to strengthen the four countries' defence industries.

Middle East Eye, citing sources, reports that joint production facilities are expected to be set up in Türkiye, which has the most developed defence industry of the four. Qatar is reported to be contributing funding and technology. The same report describes a two-level structure: technical teams draw up joint production plans, and a board of ministers and officials takes the final decisions on budgets and production sites.

## Why now

Middle East Eye reports that Qatar wants cost-effective air defence after the Iran war stretched Gulf air defences. It also notes that American systems such as Patriot and THAAD face delivery backlogs of several years. Turkish manufacturers already export drones, air-defence systems and armoured vehicles.

## The name

Türkiye's ministry says the name refers to the Battle of Badr in 624, and notes that *badr* also means "full moon", describing the four countries as complementing one another.

## What we don't know yet

A memorandum of understanding sets out intentions; it is not a mutual-defence treaty. No budget, production targets or timelines have been made public. At the time of the first reports, Pakistan's Foreign Office had not commented on the pact.
